Pragmatic has incorporated as Pragmatic Research, Inc.

Pragmatic has incorporated. The company behind the runtime is Pragmatic Research, Inc., and everything we build, license, and support now ships under that name.

Nothing about the work changes. The runtime stays in early access. The practical difference is the counterparty on licenses and agreements: Pragmatic Research, Inc.

Why Research? Mostly because it reflects how this has gone so far. The thinking came before the runtime, and the hope is to keep working in that order.

Aniketh Bandlamudi
